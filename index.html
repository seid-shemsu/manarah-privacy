Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 14 Oct 2026

Thank you for using Manarah (منارة).

This Privacy Policy explains how this app handles information.

Information We Collect

This app does not collect any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no crash reporting.

Everything you do in the app stays on your device, including:

  • Your location or chosen city
  • Settings and preferences
  • Bookmarks and reading progress
  • Azkar, tasbih and prayer progress
  • Downloaded recitations

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Location

If you choose "Use my location", the app asks your phone for your approximate location once. It uses it only to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction on your device. Your location is never sent anywhere.

You can choose a city from the list instead, and you can turn off location access at any time in your phone's settings.

Notifications

Prayer alerts and azkar reminders are scheduled on your device. No information is sent to a server to deliver them.

Advertising

This app does not display any type of ads.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use Firebase, Google Analytics, advertising networks or any other tracking service.

The only time the app connects to the internet is to stream or download Quran recitations from EveryAyah. As with any website, their server receives your device's IP address to deliver the audio. The app does not send them any other information.

If you open an external link from the About screen, it opens in your browser and that website's own privacy policy applies.

How Information Is Used

Because the app collects no information, none is used, shared or sold.

Children's Privacy

This app is suitable for all ages. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Data Security

All of your data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. We have no servers that store your information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
